# U.S. regulatory and institutional routing

Use this file only to locate authoritative sources and identify the human office that must decide. Never convert it into a compliance determination.

## Routing table

| Trigger | Authoritative starting point | Human decision owner |
|---|---|---|
| Human participants or identifiable human data/biospecimens | [HHS OHRP regulations and policy](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/index.html), [45 CFR 46](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/regulations/45-cfr-46/index.html), and, for FDA-regulated research, [21 CFR Part 56](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/part-56) | Institutional IRB, privacy office, and study sponsor |
| Recombinant or synthetic nucleic acids, engineered organisms, viral vectors | [NIH Biosafety and Biosecurity Policy](https://osp.od.nih.gov/policies/biosafety-and-biosecurity-policy/) and current NIH Guidelines | Institutional IBC and biosafety/EHS office |
| Nonclinical safety studies intended to support an FDA submission | [21 CFR Part 58](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/part-58) | Study director, independent QA unit, sponsor regulatory lead |
| Drug manufacturing or finished pharmaceuticals | [21 CFR Part 210](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/part-210), [21 CFR Part 211](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/part-211), and current FDA guidance search | Quality unit, manufacturing, validation, and regulatory affairs |
| Topic-specific FDA interpretation | [FDA Guidance Document Search](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents) | Sponsor regulatory counsel/affairs; verify draft versus final status |
| Animal research | Institution's IACUC policy and applicable federal/funder rules | IACUC and attending veterinarian |

## Mandatory distinctions

- **Law/regulation** is not the same as **agency guidance**. FDA guidance generally expresses current thinking and is normally non-binding unless it cites a binding requirement.
- **Basic exploratory research** is not automatically an FDA GLP study. Applicability is a sponsor/QA/regulatory determination; do not infer it from the presence of laboratory work alone.
- **IRB** and **IBC** cover different risks. A project can require both, either, or neither; only the institution makes the determination.
- **GLP** and **GMP** are not interchangeable. GLP addresses qualifying nonclinical study conduct and data integrity; GMP addresses controlled manufacturing and product quality.
- A citation to a rule does not prove compliance. Compliance depends on the current rule, study context, institutional policies, controlled records, and accountable human review.

## Safe output format

For a regulatory question, return:

1. the activity and jurisdiction assumed;
2. potentially relevant authoritative sources with retrieval date;
3. why each source may be relevant;
4. unresolved applicability questions;
5. the institutional office or accountable professional who must decide;
6. a clear statement that no compliance, legal, medical, or quality-unit decision was made.
