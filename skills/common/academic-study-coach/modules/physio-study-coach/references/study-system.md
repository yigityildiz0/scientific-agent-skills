# FTR study system

## Source fidelity labels

- `KAYNAKTA VAR`: directly stated or clearly shown in supplied material; retain page/slide locator.
- `AÇIKLAMA`: a pedagogical explanation or inference added to make the source understandable.
- `GÜNCEL EK BİLGİ`: externally researched context; cite it and do not imply the lecturer included it.
- `OKUNAMIYOR/EKSİK`: text or image cannot be recovered reliably; request a clearer page when decisive.

## High-yield extraction grid

For each topic, look for:

| Domain | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Definition/classification | What is it, and what is it often confused with? |
| Mechanism | Why do the findings occur? |
| Presentation | Which signs/symptoms and patterns matter? |
| Assessment | What is measured, how, and with what limits? |
| Clinical reasoning | Which finding changes the hypothesis or plan? |
| Intervention | Goal, indication, contraindication, dose variable, progression, stop rule |
| Outcome | What changes, by how much, and when is it reassessed? |
| Safety | Red flags, precautions, referral, population-specific limits |

## Retrieval design

Use a mix of:

- one-fact recall for terminology and thresholds;
- compare/contrast for similar diagnoses, tests, stages, or interventions;
- ordered steps for assessment and management sequences;
- “why” questions for mechanisms;
- short cases that require selecting and justifying the next step;
- error detection: identify what is unsafe, unsupported, or missing.

Keep one main testable idea per flashcard. Answers should be short enough to grade. Include an explanation only when the distinction matters.

## Quiz quality

- Distractors must be plausible and mutually distinct.
- Do not reveal the correct answer through length, grammar, or repeated wording.
- For multiple choice, explain why the correct option wins and why each distractor fails.
- For open questions, provide a compact scoring rubric based on supplied content.
- Label every generated case as fictional/educational.

## OSCE pattern

Define station goal, candidate instructions, available information, expected sequence, communication/safety behaviors, key findings, scoring checklist, critical fail, and examiner prompts. Do not include patient-specific high-risk procedures as unsupervised practice instructions.

## Review loop

After the learner answers, classify errors as knowledge gap, confusion, recall failure, reasoning failure, or careless reading. Re-teach only the failed distinction, then retest it in a different form. Increase spacing after correct retrieval; shorten it after errors.
