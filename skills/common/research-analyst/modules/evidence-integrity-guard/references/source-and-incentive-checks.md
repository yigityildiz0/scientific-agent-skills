# Source quality and incentive checks

Judge a source by fitness for the exact claim, not by a universal prestige score.

## Source routing

- **Rules, status, specifications, filings, labels, prices, schedules:** the responsible regulator, registry, exchange, issuer, manufacturer, standards body, or service's official current record.
- **Scientific and clinical effects:** registered protocols, peer-reviewed primary studies, systematic reviews, guidelines, regulators, and trial registries. Match population, intervention, comparator, outcome, and duration.
- **Company and investment facts:** filings, exchange notices, audited reports, fund documents, regulator records, and timestamped market data; use independent analysis for interpretation.
- **Product performance and value:** official specifications for identity and warranty, independent standardized testing for performance, multiple retailers for current price, and owner reports only for recurring real-world failure modes.
- **News and disputed events:** original documents, direct records, on-record reporting, and several genuinely independent reputable outlets.

Official does not mean neutral: an issuer is primary for what it filed or announced, not independent proof that its marketing interpretation is correct.

## Incentive flags

Record, when visible:

- `SELLER_OR_ISSUER`: profits directly from the claim;
- `AFFILIATE`: earns commission from purchase links or leads;
- `SPONSORED`: content or study funded by an interested party;
- `ADVOCACY`: mission creates a directional incentive;
- `AD_SUPPORTED`: incentives favor attention or volume;
- `UNDISCLOSED_OR_UNKNOWN`: conflicts cannot be assessed;
- `INDEPENDENT`: no material interest found, not a guarantee of accuracy.

Interested sources can establish identity, declared features, or the party's position. Do not use them alone for superiority, effectiveness, safety, value, or broad causal claims. Funding or sponsorship is a risk-of-bias signal, not an automatic exclusion: inspect methods, outcome selection, data access, funder role and independent replication. Remove promotional recommendations from the evidence chain unless their factual content is independently supported. An undisclosed conflict is unknown, not proof of independence.

## Advertising and manipulation signals

Treat these as reasons for more verification, not automatic proof of falsehood:

- advertorial or sponsored labels, coupon codes, affiliate disclosures, seller ownership;
- only positive comparisons, vague “expert approved” language, unnamed studies;
- percentages without denominator, timeframe, comparator, or source;
- urgency, scarcity, countdowns, “clinically proven” without an exact trial;
- before/after images without protocol, blinding, or representative sampling;
- review pages whose products, rankings, and outbound merchants change together;
- repeated wording across nominally different sites, indicating syndication or SEO copying.

## Practical source matrix

For each decisive claim, aim for:

1. one source that directly owns or generated the relevant evidence;
2. one independent source that interprets or corroborates it when practical;
3. one deliberate counterevidence search.

If only an interested source exists, label the statement as the party's claim and cap confidence.
