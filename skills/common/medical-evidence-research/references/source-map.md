# Source Map

Choose sources by function and topic. Verify current access, coverage, and syntax on the official site at search time.

## User-supplied discovery guides

- [Youthall's five-site introduction](https://blog.youthall.com/bilimsel-makalelere-ulasmak-icin-kullanabileceginiz-5-web-sitesi/) points to Google Scholar, YÖK Thesis, Academia.edu, DergiPark and ResearchGate. Use it as an introductory access map, not as clinical evidence or a systematic-search protocol. Verify each found work's DOI/record, version, review status and methods; author/profile scores and social-network popularity are not evidence-quality measures.
- [Marmara GEMHAM literature-search page](https://gemham.marmara.edu.tr/hizmetler/literatur-tarama) is an institutional starting point. Recheck its current links and access requirements when using it; an institutional link page is not itself a database search result or a clinical recommendation. If its contents cannot be retrieved, report that limitation rather than inventing its list.
- [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/) is directly searchable journal infrastructure, already covered below. Verify the exact journal, indexed years, article type, conflicts, corrections/retractions and original record. Its presence there is not proof of methodological quality.

These guides supplement the biomedical/rehabilitation databases below. Translate the clinical question into a reproducible query, keep a dated search log, deduplicate, screen against declared eligibility criteria and appraise the selected studies before making a conclusion.

## Core biomedical and rehabilitation sources

| Source | Function | Best use | Access and caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) / MEDLINE | Biomedical citation database; MEDLINE records use MeSH | Broad biomedical and clinical searching | Free. PubMed is not itself a full-text database; follow publisher or PMC links. |
| [PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) | Full-text archive | Reading eligible biomedical articles | Free full text, but inclusion does not make every item equivalent in design or quality. |
| [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/) | Literature and full-text repository with grants, citations, and preprints | Open full text, citation links, life-science discovery | Free. Label preprints separately from peer-reviewed papers. |
| [Embase](https://www.elsevier.com/products/embase) | Biomedical and pharmacological database using Emtree | Systematic reviews, drugs, devices, adverse effects, conference coverage | Usually subscription-based. Report lack of access and provide an Embase-ready query. |
| [Cochrane Library](https://www.cochranelibrary.com/) | Cochrane reviews and CENTRAL trial records | Evidence syntheses and controlled-trial discovery | Search access varies; some full text may require subscription. |
| [PEDro](https://pedro.org.au/) | Physiotherapy trials, systematic reviews, and clinical practice guidelines | Intervention evidence in physiotherapy and rehabilitation | Free. PEDro scores support rapid trial appraisal but do not replace outcome-specific judgment. |
| [DiTA](https://dita.org.au/) | Diagnostic test accuracy evidence relevant to physiotherapy | Rehabilitation-related diagnostic questions | Use for diagnosis, not as the main intervention database. |
| [CINAHL](https://about.ebsco.com/products/research-databases/cinahl-database) | Nursing and allied-health index | Rehabilitation, nursing, occupational therapy, patient education, qualitative evidence | Usually institutional subscription; use CINAHL Subject Headings. |
| [SPORTDiscus](https://about.ebsco.com/products/research-databases/sportdiscus) | Sport and exercise literature | Exercise science, athletic performance, sports rehabilitation | Usually subscription-based. |
| [AMED](https://about.ebsco.com/products/research-databases/amed-allied-and-complementary-medicine-database) | Allied and complementary medicine index | Therapies underrepresented in mainstream biomedical databases | Usually subscription-based; appraise study quality carefully. |
| [PsycINFO](https://www.apa.org/pubs/databases/psycinfo) | Behavioral and psychological literature | Pain, adherence, behavior, mental health, qualitative topics | Usually subscription-based. |

## Multidisciplinary discovery and citation tools

| Source | Function | Use it for | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Scopus](https://www.elsevier.com/products/scopus) | Abstract and citation database | Multidisciplinary discovery, forward citations, author/journal verification | Usually subscription-based. Verify journal coverage in Scopus Sources, including active years. |
| [Web of Science](https://www.webofscience.com/) | Citation-index platform | Citation chaining and multidisciplinary searching | Usually subscription-based. Verify journals in the official Master Journal List. |
| [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) | Broad scholarly search engine | Supplementary discovery, theses, repositories, citation chasing | Coverage and ranking are opaque; do not use as the sole reproducible database for formal reviews. |
| [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) | Open scholarly catalog and citation graph | Broad discovery, citation networks, bibliometrics, open metadata | Useful for discovery; verify decisive bibliographic details with the original record or publisher. |
| [Crossref Metadata Search](https://search.crossref.org/) | DOI and scholarly metadata lookup | DOI verification, title/author matching, updates and metadata | Metadata quality depends on deposits; it is not a clinical evidence-quality filter. |
| [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) | Scholarly discovery and citation graph | Related-paper discovery and citation exploration | Use as a supplement and verify against primary records. |

## Turkish literature and research infrastructure

| Source | Function | Use it for | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| [TR Dizin](https://trdizin.gov.tr/) | TÜBİTAK ULAKBİM national journal index and search database | Turkish peer-reviewed literature and official TR Dizin coverage checks | Verify the exact journal and indexed years. |
| [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/) | Journal hosting, article access, and editorial-process infrastructure | Searching hosted Turkish journals and submitting to a selected hosted journal | Presence on DergiPark is not the same as TR Dizin, MEDLINE, Scopus, or Web of Science indexing and is not by itself a quality verdict. |
| [YÖK National Thesis Center](https://tez.yok.gov.tr/UlusalTezMerkezi/) | Turkish theses and dissertations | Grey literature, local populations, unpublished or detailed methods | Check access permission, embargo, degree type, institution, and whether findings were later published. |
| [Harman](https://harman.ulakbim.gov.tr/) | Aggregator for Turkish institutional repositories | Repository copies, theses, reports, and other open outputs | Repository inclusion does not imply peer review. |
| [Aperta](https://aperta.ulakbim.gov.tr/) | TÜBİTAK ULAKBİM open repository with DOI and versioning | Data, research outputs, protocols, and citable deposits | Treat repository deposits according to their stated review status; do not assume peer review. |

Search Turkish sources with Turkish and English terminology, diacritic variants, former disease names, abbreviations, and institution-specific terminology when relevant.

## Registries and unpublished or ongoing evidence

| Source | Function | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) | Clinical study registry and results database | Ongoing, completed, unpublished, terminated, or selectively reported studies |
| [WHO ICTRP](https://trialsearch.who.int/) | Portal across WHO-recognized trial registries | International trial discovery beyond a single registry |
| [ISRCTN](https://www.isrctn.com/) | Clinical study registry | Registered trials and protocols |
| [EU CTIS](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/) | European Union clinical-trial information system | EU clinical trials and public documents |
| [PROSPERO](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/) | Prospective systematic-review registry | Existing or ongoing reviews, protocol comparison, duplication checks |
| [OSF Registries](https://osf.io/registries) | General research registration platform | Protocols and registrations not covered elsewhere |

Compare registry outcomes and analysis plans with the published paper when selective reporting is plausible. A registry entry is not proof that results are valid or complete.

## Guidelines and evidence summaries

- Search [WHO](https://www.who.int/publications/i), [NICE](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance), national health authorities, and relevant professional societies for current guidelines.
- Use [TRIP](https://www.tripdatabase.com/) and guideline repositories as discovery aids, then verify the issuing body, methods, evidence dates, conflicts, and update status.
- Prefer guidelines with explicit systematic-search and recommendation-development methods. Do not treat all society statements as equivalent.

## Preprints, repositories, and open access

- Use [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/) for health-science preprints and [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/) for biology preprints.
- Use [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) to discover open-access journals and articles.
- Use institutional repositories, PMC, Europe PMC, or legal open-access resolvers to locate full text.
- Label preprints as not peer reviewed, check for later journal publication, compare versions, and never let a preprint alone drive clinical care.

## Journal selection and verification

### Publisher platforms and author copies

| Source | Function | Use and verification |
|---|---|---|
| [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/) | Elsevier publisher platform for journals/books and full text | Locate publisher versions and supplementary material; access varies. It is distinct from Scopus and does not replace a broad clinical database. |
| [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) / [Academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/) | Researcher profiles and author-uploaded copies | Discover legal copies or an author contact. Match title/authors/DOI to the authoritative record; identify preprint, accepted manuscript or version of record and check corrections/retractions. Availability and permission vary. |

Author/profile scores, citation counts, h-index, journal metrics and platform presence do not establish a paper's validity or clinical usefulness. Appraise its design, target result, effect/uncertainty and applicability. General blog/university link lists can suggest sources, but verify current methods in the official database help rather than treating a link list as a search protocol.

### Verification sources

| Need | Preferred verification source |
|---|---|
| MEDLINE status | [NLM Catalog](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/) |
| Scopus coverage | [Scopus Sources](https://www.scopus.com/sources) |
| Web of Science coverage | [Master Journal List](https://mjl.clarivate.com/) |
| Journal Impact Factor | [Journal Citation Reports](https://jcr.clarivate.com/) |
| Scopus-based public indicators | [SCImago Journal & Country Rank](https://www.scimagojr.com/) |
| TR Dizin coverage | [TR Dizin journal search](https://search.trdizin.gov.tr/tr/dergi/ara) |
| Open-access directory | [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) |
| Publishing ethics guidance | [COPE](https://publicationethics.org/) and [ICMJE](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/) |
| Suspicious-journal screening | [Think. Check. Submit.](https://thinkchecksubmit.org/journals/) |
| Reporting guideline | [EQUATOR Network](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/) |

Indexing claims, quartiles, and metrics can change. Check the official source live and record the date. A metric or index does not replace scope fit, methods, ethics, and editorial quality.

## Submission and publishing infrastructure

- [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/), [Open Journal Systems (OJS)](https://pkp.sfu.ca/software/ojs/), [ScholarOne Manuscripts](https://www.silverchair.com/products/scholarone-manuscripts/), and [Editorial Manager](https://www.ariessys.com/solutions/editorial-manager/) manage submission or editorial workflows.
- Use only the submission link reached from the journal's official website.
- Do not infer journal quality from the platform it uses.
- Never upload a manuscript, approve a generated submission PDF, accept charges, transfer copyright, or finalize submission without explicit user authorization.

Use [ORCID](https://orcid.org/) for persistent researcher identity; use Crossref or DataCite metadata to verify publication or dataset identifiers. Use OSF, Zenodo, institutional repositories, or Aperta only according to the output type, consent, data sensitivity, and target journal policy.
