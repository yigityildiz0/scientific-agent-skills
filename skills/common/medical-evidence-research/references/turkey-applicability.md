# International evidence and applicability to Türkiye

Use a broad international evidence search first; a Türkiye-only sample is not the default eligibility criterion. Add Turkish and English local searches for applicability, measurement and implementation, then distinguish the global effect estimate from the local implementation judgment.

## Two linked search tracks

- Global: PubMed/MEDLINE + PEDro for rehabilitation interventions; DiTA for diagnostic accuracy; Cochrane/guidelines, trial registries and citation chasing as relevant. Translate controlled vocabulary and syntax per platform, not by copying one query everywhere. Do not exclude all non-English studies or add a country filter without a reason.
- Türkiye: TR Dizin, DergiPark and YÖK Tez using both Turkish/English terms, Türkçe/Turkish/Türkiye/Turkey, the condition, intervention and outcome measure. Confirm actual participant location; author affiliation is not the study population. Link thesis/article/registry reports to one study so participants are not counted twice.
- Use public and already available no-extra-cost access. Record unavailable subscription databases and inaccessible full texts as coverage gaps; no new subscription, school-account connection or billed discovery service. A paywall is not an exclusion criterion for relevance.

## Applicability matrix (only factors that matter to the question)

For each dimension record international study context, Türkiye context, evidence/source, effect on applicability and remaining unknown:

1. Population: diagnosis definition, age, stage/severity, comorbidity and baseline risk. Do not equate nationality with biological effect modification.
2. Care setting/comparator: inpatient/outpatient/home, usual-care content, referral access, therapist training, supervision and co-interventions.
3. Intervention: actual frequency/intensity/time/type, progression, adherence/fidelity, equipment, staffing and adverse-event monitoring. Separate the studied dose from a suggested adaptation.
4. Outcomes: exact Turkish scale version, validation population, validity/reliability/responsiveness, scoring direction and license. Translation alone is not validation; MIC/MCID and MDC/SEM are not interchangeable and may not transfer between populations.
5. Feasibility/equity: language, health literacy, cultural acceptability, travel/digital access, caregiver support, cost and SGK/service availability. Verify current SGK/SUT rules only when they affect the decision; do not invent reimbursement.
6. Certainty: explain material indirectness separately from risk of bias/imprecision. A foreign study is not automatically inapplicable; a Turkish study is not automatically superior.

Conclude: directly applicable / applicable with specified adaptations / uncertain / not applicable, with reasons. An untested adaptation remains a hypothesis; say what local data or pilot would change the conclusion. Preserve clinician verification for patient-specific application.

## Sponsorship and synthesis

Record funder role, author conflicts, registration/outcome changes and access to data. Do not automatically discard funded studies. Judge methods and independent confirmation; seller claims alone do not establish effectiveness. Compare effect sizes, CI, follow-up, harms and outcome-specific certainty rather than counting positive papers.
